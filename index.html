Flagship

Solafide (SOAS)

Live in production

An OHADA-compliant ERP for Francophone Africa

Solafide is a full accounting & business platform built on Django and django-ledger, implementing the SYSCOHADA 2018 chart of accounts. It runs as a multi-tenant SaaS, ships as an offline-first desktop application, and is fully bilingual (French default / English). Everything — the ledger, POS, payroll, inventory, reporting — is engineered for financial correctness and for real businesses on real networks in the CEMAC region.

Under the hood

What I've built inside Solafide

Real modules shipping in production — each one a full sub-system.

General Ledger & Accounting

SYSCOHADA 2018 chart of accounts, double-entry journals, reversals, and financial statements (DSF).

Point of Sale

Offline-capable POS with sequence integrity, receipts, and always-connected UX for spotty networks.

HRM & Payroll

Employee records, competencies, document management, and payroll wired into the ledger.

Inventory & Stock

Mixed-unit UoM (base-unit storage + multipliers), cost-basis auditing, and multi-branch scope.

Mobile Money & Payments

MoMo statement import, payment-provider auditing, and reconciliation in XAF (FCFA).

Multi-tenant SaaS

Isolated tenants, entitlement & add-on modules, and hosted feature gating per environment.

AI Assistance

Claude & OpenAI integrations for guided workflows, document handling, and automation.

RBAC & Audit

Granular roles, separation-of-duties, superuser access grants, and full change history.

Offline Desktop Build

Packaged desktop app with a pre-migrated seed DB for instant first run — no internet required.
